What we do

Better experiences start with better testing.

From small websites to complex platforms, we identify issues that get in the way of real people using your digital service.

UI testing

Identify confusing navigation, broken interactions, inconsistent layouts, and mobile usability issues.

Accessibility audits

Review against relevant WCAG 2.2 criteria, including keyboard access, text alternatives, forms, and contrast.

UX reviews

Evaluate user journeys, content clarity, form flows, and design decisions that affect task completion.

Fix recommendations

Receive prioritized issues, clear explanations, and practical next steps for designers and developers.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Need something more specific? Email sales@humanfirst.digital.

Do you provide an accessibility certification?

No. Our reviews provide findings and recommendations against relevant accessibility criteria. An audit is not a guarantee of legal compliance, and it is not an official certification.

What types of digital services can you review?

We can discuss marketing websites, education platforms, online forms, dashboards, web applications, and other digital interfaces. Scope and test coverage depend on the selected package.

Will you fix the issues you find?

Standard packages focus on testing, reporting, and recommendations. Implementation or extended remediation support can be discussed as a custom project.

How do I get started?

Select a package and send us an email with your website URL, the pages or flows to review, and any specific concerns. We will confirm the scope before work begins.
